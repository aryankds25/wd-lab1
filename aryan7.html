About

Tony Stark is a fictional superhero appearing in Marvel Comics. He is a brilliant engineer and inventor who builds a powered suit of armor to save his life and becomes Iron Man.

Achievements

  • Created Iron Man suit
  • Founded Avengers
  • Saved Earth multiple times
  • Invented advanced AI systems like JARVIS

Legacy

Tony Stark sacrificed himself to defeat Thanos and save the universe. His legacy continues as a symbol of intelligence, courage, and innovation.
